Sponsorship Tier Realignment

Samsung has transitioned to the Gold Package (2 Homepage Takeover Days, 2 Weeks Top Strip, and full Gold asset suite). Shark Ninja is out of the campaign, opening premium takeover days for new sponsor activations.

Gold Package Factsheet Inclusions

Gold Package (Samsung, Zinox, Midea, LG, ASUS, Anker, Vitali) includes: Homepage Takeover (2 days), Top Strip Banner (2 weeks), Category Banner (4 weeks), Dedicated Newsletter (3 pushes), App & Browser Push (3 pushes), Hot Picks Video (1 video), Konga FM (1 spot), and Sponsored Posts (2 units).

Brand Day Package (Guinness & Elepaq)

Guinness (Nov 18) and Elepaq (Nov 11) are confirmed on the Brand Day Package, receiving dedicated brand day takeovers, category banner placement, push notifications, and social features.

Available Sponsor Opportunities

With Shark Ninja out, Samsung on Gold, and Fridays dedicated to Yakata Live events, remaining open campaign days and weekend top strip carryover positions are available for additional brand packages.

Silver Package Factsheet Inclusions

Silver Package (Kenwood, MamaHome, Carrier & Mel) delivers 4 focused inclusions: Category Banner (2 weeks), Newsletter Banner (2 pushes), Deals Page Feature (10 products × 3 days), and 1 Organic Social Post.
